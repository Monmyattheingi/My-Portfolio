01 / Problem

Clinical text is useful—but unstructured.

Clinical notes combine symptoms, diagnoses, medicines, dosage, duration, and negated findings inside ordinary sentences.

This prototype extracts the important elements, connects nearby medication details, and checks medicine pairs against a curated local interaction dataset.

02 / Workflow

From note to structured result.

Step 01Enter

Paste a free-text clinical observation.

Step 02Extract

Identify problems, tests, medicines, dosage, and duration.

Step 03Relate

Connect nearby medication and dosage mentions.

Step 04Check

Compare medicine pairs and surface warnings.
