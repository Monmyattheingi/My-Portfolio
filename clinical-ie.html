01 / Problem

Clinical text is useful—but unstructured.

Clinical notes mix symptoms, diagnoses, medicines, dosage, duration, and negated findings in ordinary sentences.

This prototype extracts the important elements and compares medication pairs against a curated local interaction dataset.

02 / How it works

From note to structured result

Step 1Enter note

Paste clinical observations.

Step 2Extract

Identify problems, tests, medicines, dosage, and duration.

Step 3Relate

Link nearby medication and dosage mentions.

Step 4Check

Compare medicine pairs and display warnings.
