01 / Problem

Legal answers need evidence, not confidence alone.

Semantic search can retrieve text that sounds relevant without proving which law, section, or legal status supports an answer.

Legal Insight centers traceability: reviewed sources, versioned ingestion, hybrid retrieval, exact citations, and an “insufficient evidence” response when support is weak.

02 / Architecture

A grounded retrieval pipeline

Reviewed PDF → Text / OCR → Section-aware chunks → BGE-M3 embeddings → PostgreSQL + pgvector
User question → Hybrid retrieval → Grounded answer + sources

03 / Key decisions

Designed for responsible retrieval

Reviewed ingestion

A controlled document queue avoids silently collecting unverified legal material.

Source visibility

Results expose law name, section, status, and source URL.

Honest fallback

Weak retrieval returns insufficient evidence instead of inventing a legal conclusion.

Important: Legal information for research—not a substitute for professional legal advice.

04 / Stack

Built across the full pipeline

Retrieval: multilingual dense embeddings plus keyword search.

Data: PostgreSQL, pgvector, document hashing, and section-aware metadata.

Automation: n8n ingestion and question-answering workflows with OCR support.

Quality: human-reviewed evaluation questions and grounded-answer checks.
