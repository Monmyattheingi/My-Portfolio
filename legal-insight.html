01 / Problem

Legal answers need evidence—not confidence alone.

Semantic search can retrieve text that sounds relevant without proving which law, section, or legal status supports an answer.

Legal Insight prioritizes traceability through reviewed sources, section-aware ingestion, hybrid retrieval, exact citations, and an insufficient-evidence response when support is weak.

03 / Decisions

Designed for responsible retrieval.

Input quality

Reviewed ingestion

A controlled document queue avoids silently collecting unverified legal material.

Traceability

Visible sources

Results expose the law name, section, status, and source URL.

Reliability

Honest fallback

Weak retrieval returns insufficient evidence instead of inventing a conclusion.

Scope: Legal information for research—not a substitute for professional legal advice.
